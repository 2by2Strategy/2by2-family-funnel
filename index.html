    Then, every step after

    • Before your first meeting, your counselor receives everything we learned on the matchmaking call: your student’s goals, grades, school list so far, and what’s keeping you up at night. Your student never has to start over or repeat their story. Day one starts at step two.

    • Family and counselor work from the same live plan: every deadline, checkpoint and goal in one place, synced to your calendar. You always know what’s done, what’s next and who owns it, from the day you join to decision day.

    • The match isn’t where we leave. We check in with your family throughout the process, and if anything feels off, from slow essay feedback to a missed deadline, we step back in for your student and make it right.

    • Any admissions question, any hour, free. Ask 2by2 gives straight answers in seconds on lists, essays, testing and aid, so small questions never wait for the next meeting. Everything you ask helps your counselor understand your student better.

    • A short note only when something changes that affects your student: test policies, financial aid rules, new deadlines. No spam and no pitch. Joining the 2by2 family puts you on it.
