About 2by2 Academy

2by2 Academy is a college admissions matchmaking service operated by 2by2 Strategy LLC, a New York limited liability company.

What we do

We help families find the right independent college counselor for their student. Instead of selling a one-size-fits-all package, we get to know the student, then match them with a vetted independent educational consultant whose experience fits the student’s goals, schools and budget.

We work with high school students applying to college, as well as transfer applicants.

How it works

  1. Free matchmaking call. A 30-minute call where a 2by2 matchmaker learns about your student: goals, grades, interests and concerns.
  2. Your match. We recommend the independent counselor from our network who fits your student best, and explain why.
  3. Meet before you commit. Your student meets the counselor before your family makes any financial decision.
  4. Briefing and handoff. The counselor receives a full brief, so your student never has to start over.
  5. Ongoing support. We check in with your family through the process and step in if anything needs fixing.

Our counselor network

Every counselor in our network is an independent educational consultant who has applied, interviewed with our team and been vetted on their record and specialties. Counselors in the network work at different price points, and your family will know the cost before committing to anything.

How we communicate

We reply to families by email and phone. Families who opt in on our Text us page or on a form on our website also receive text messages from 2by2 Strategy LLC about their inquiry: answers to their questions, follow-ups, and matchmaking call scheduling, confirmations and reminders. We do not send promotional texts. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See our Terms and Privacy Policy.
