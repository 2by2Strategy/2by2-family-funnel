Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 2by2 Strategy LLC (“2by2,” “2by2 Academy,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses and protects information when you visit 2by2academy.co, use our chat or matchmaking questionnaire, or communicate with us by text, phone or email. 2by2 Academy is a brand of 2by2 Strategy LLC.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, mobile phone number, your student’s first name, grade, school, ZIP code, and answers or questions you share through our forms, chat or questionnaire.
  • Communication records: messages you send us by text, email or chat, and your text-messaging consent choices.
  • Basic usage information: pages visited and how you arrived (for example, ad campaign tags in the link), collected so we can understand which outreach is useful.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and follow up about your inquiry.
  • To schedule, confirm and remind you about your matchmaking call.
  • To recommend and introduce an independent college counselor who fits your student, if you ask us to.
  • To send the 2by2 Admissions Letter and updates you sign up for (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To operate, secure and improve our website and services.

Text messaging (SMS)

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

If you opt in to text messages, we use your mobile number only to communicate with you about your inquiry, your matchmaking call, and related 2by2 services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. See our Terms for full SMS terms.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With the independent counselor we match you with, and only after you ask us to make that introduction. Counselors receive only the information needed for that introduction. We do not share your mobile number or text-messaging consent with counselors for their marketing.
  • With service providers that help us run 2by2 (for example, our CRM, scheduling, email, text messaging and data-storage providers), who may use it only to provide services to us.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of 2by2, our users or others.

The sharing described above never includes mobile numbers or text-messaging opt-in data for anyone’s marketing purposes.

Our map

If you join the 2by2 family, you may be counted on our public map. The map only shows state-level counts. We never show your name, school or contact information to other families.

Students and children

Our services are intended for parents and guardians and for students working with them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop text messages.
  • Use the unsubscribe link in any email to stop emails.
  • Email us to access, correct or delete your information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page with an updated date.
